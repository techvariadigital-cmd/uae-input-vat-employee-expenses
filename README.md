# UAE Input VAT on Employee Expenses: A Practical Guide for 2026

Employee expenses are a routine part of business operations, but recovering input VAT on these costs requires careful assessment. Transport, meals, accommodation, mobile phones, internet services, and parking may support business activities, yet not every employee-related expense qualifies for VAT recovery.
FTA Decision No. 17 of 2026 introduces specific conditions for recovering input VAT on certain goods and services provided to employees free of charge. Effective from 1 October 2026, the decision makes it important for businesses in the UAE to review employee benefits, reimbursement policies, and supporting records.

## Which Employee Expenses May Qualify?

The decision addresses six categories, each with distinct requirements.

### 1. Employee Transport
Work-related transport between home and work, client premises, or locations directly connected with employment duties may qualify when the relevant conditions are satisfied. The availability of a cash allowance alternative can affect the assessment.

### 2. Food and Beverages
Specific conditions apply to employees working in remote, distant, or isolated locations where suitable food preparation facilities are unavailable and restaurants are not easily accessible. Businesses should retain evidence explaining the work location and the need for the arrangement.

### 3. Employee Accommodation
Accommodation must meet the applicable operational and residential conditions. Personal or family use and significant recreational benefits can affect the VAT assessment, so businesses should review each arrangement carefully.

### 4. Temporary Accommodation for New Employees
Temporary accommodation is covered separately, subject to a limit of up to 30 days and conditions relating to suitability and basic residency needs. Records should establish the purpose and duration of the stay.

### 5. Mobile Phones and Internet Services
Mobile phones, airtime, data, and home internet must meet the relevant work-purpose requirements. Documented usage policies, reasonable monitoring arrangements, and records of unauthorised use are important considerations.

### 6. Business Parking
Parking expenses must meet the applicable business-purpose requirements. Payment evidence should show the date, time, amount, and tax paid, alongside an appropriate reimbursement and approval process.

Not every employee benefit qualifies for input VAT recovery. Businesses must assess each expense against the conditions applicable to its category.

## Why Expense Documentation Matters?
A receipt confirms that a payment was made, but it may not explain why the expense was necessary or whether the requirements for VAT recovery have been met.

A reliable employee expense management process should include:

- Expense categories based on the purpose of each cost.
- Receipts and supporting evidence collected when claims are submitted.
- Appropriate approval procedures for reimbursements.
- Written policies for relevant employee benefits and communication expenses.
- Records supporting business use and accommodation arrangements.
- Regular reviews of expense classifications before VAT returns are filed.

Finance teams should coordinate with HR, IT, and operations because supporting evidence may be held outside the accounting department.

## How Accounting Systems Can Support Compliance?
Digital systems can help organize receipts, approval records, and accounting entries. UAE Zoho Expenses may support expense submission and approval workflows, while Zoho Books can help maintain accounting records when configured for the business's requirements.

Businesses operating through several legal entities should also review their multi-entity accounting procedures. Each expense, invoice, and supporting document should be associated with the correct entity, with clear responsibility for review and approval.

However, accounting software does not automatically determine whether an expense qualifies for input VAT recovery. Businesses must assess the applicable FTA regulations and the facts surrounding each transaction.

## What Should Businesses Review?
Start by reviewing employee benefit arrangements, particularly cash alternatives for transport, food and beverages, and accommodation.

Next, identify missing receipts, incomplete payment evidence, and policies that do not clearly explain permitted use or approval requirements. Make sure finance, HR, IT, and operations understand their responsibilities for maintaining the relevant records.

Finally, consider the effective date. FTA Decision No. 17 of 2026 takes effect from 1 October 2026. Earlier expenses should not automatically be assessed using the new wording without considering the rules applicable to those transactions.

Where eligibility is uncertain, a qualified UAE tax adviser should review the specific circumstances.

## Conclusion
Input VAT recovery on employee expenses depends on more than whether a cost is business-related. Businesses need to understand the conditions for each category, retain appropriate evidence, and apply consistent review procedures.

Clear policies and reliable accounting records can support this process, but they do not guarantee VAT recovery. Careful assessment remains essential for effective UAE VAT compliance.
